App Name: Butu
Developer: Omega Farms / Adolf Ambe Iyase

How to Delete Your Butu Account

To permanently delete your account and all associated data from our servers, please follow these steps:

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Create a new email addressed to: iyase.ambe@gmail.com
  3. In the subject line, write: "Account Deletion Request".
  4. In the body of the email, state the Email Address used to register your Butu account.
  5. Send the email. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Data Deleted Upon Request

  • Your Firebase Authentication Account (Email & Password hash) linked to Butu.
  • All Farming Records stored in Firestore (Harvests, Sales, Expenses).
  • Any locally cached preferences on your device (cleared upon uninstallation).

Data Retained

  • Anonymized aggregate statistics (if any exist) may be retained for service improvement, but no personally identifiable information (PII) will remain.
