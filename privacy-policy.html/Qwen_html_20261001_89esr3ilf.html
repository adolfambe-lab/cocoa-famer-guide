Privacy Policy for Cocoa Farmer Guide

Last Updated: October 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Cocoa Farmer Guide ("the App"), developed by Omega Farms. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

2. Information We Collect

We collect minimal data necessary to provide core features:

  • Location Data: We access your device's precise location (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION) solely to fetch accurate local weather forecasts for your farm area. This data is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers.
  • Firebase Authentication: If you choose to register, we store your email address securely via Firebase Auth to manage your account and sync farming records.
  • Farming Records: Any harvest or expense data you enter is stored in your private Firestore database. It is visible only to you.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display hyper-local weather conditions and spraying advice.
  • To synchronize your farming logs across devices if you log in.
  • We do NOT sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties for advertising purposes.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services which have their own privacy policies:

  • Open-Meteo API: Provides weather data. No personal info is sent to them other than coordinates.
  • Yahoo Finance API: Provides global cocoa price trends.
  • Google Firebase: Used for authentication and cloud storage.

5. Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures to protect your data. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure. By using the App, you acknowledge these risks.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is intended for adult farmers. We do not knowingly collect data from individuals under the age of 13.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
